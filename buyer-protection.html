Buyer Protection

Buying food and livestock online should feel safe. Every order on Bouesti Marketplace is covered by the protections below, from the moment you order until you have received and checked your goods.

Full-refund promise

If your order is not delivered, or what arrives does not match the listing, you get a full refund.

Livestock health certificate

Every livestock order comes with an up-to-date vet health certificate.

24-hour claims desk

Report any problem within 24 hours of delivery and our team takes it up with the farm for you.

What is covered

  • Not delivered. Your order never arrives, or is cancelled by us after you have paid.
  • Not as described. The wrong item, the wrong weight or pack size, a different breed or type from the one you picked (for example white yam instead of yellow yam), or live delivered when you ordered processed (or the other way round).
  • Poor quality on arrival. Produce that is spoiled, rotten or badly damaged when it reaches you, and eggs that arrive mostly broken.
  • Livestock problems. Animals that arrive sick or injured, or that do not match their health certificate.
  • Wrong charges. You were charged more than the total shown at checkout.

How you are protected at every step

  1. Before you buy. Every listing shows clear pictures (tap to zoom), the unit or weight options and a description, so you know exactly what you are choosing. Livestock is vet-checked before it is listed.
  2. At checkout. You see the full total, including the service fee and the delivery fee for your state, before you place the order. You can choose pay on delivery.
  3. While it travels. Use Track order to follow your goods on the map once they are on the road.
  4. When it arrives. Check your goods while the rider is with you. If something is wrong, tell the rider and contact us straight away.
  5. If something is wrong. Claim within 24 hours (see below) and we handle it with the farm on your behalf.

How to make a claim

  1. Within 24 hours of delivery, message us on WhatsApp (07086087995) or email successjomiloju@gmail.com.
  2. Send your order reference (for example BM-20261006-1234), a short description of the problem, and clear photos or a short video.
  3. Our team reviews it and coordinates directly with the farm. You do not have to chase the seller.
  4. If your claim is approved, you receive a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer where stock allows.
Why 24 hours?

Fresh produce and live animals change quickly. A short claim window lets us check the problem while it is still clear and protects the honest farmers who supply you.

What is not covered

  • Claims reported more than 24 hours after delivery.
  • Damage or spoilage that happens after delivery because of storage or handling (for example fresh vegetables left in the sun).
  • Natural variation that is normal for farm produce, such as small differences in size or shape.
  • Orders that could not be delivered because the address was wrong or the phone number could not be reached.

Staying safe

  • Keep your order reference and check goods before the rider leaves.
  • Only pay through the options shown at checkout. Never send money to a personal account because someone asked you to in a chat.
  • If anything feels wrong, message us on WhatsApp.
