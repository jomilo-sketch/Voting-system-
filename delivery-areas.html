Why we started in Ekiti

Bouesti Marketplace grew out of Ekiti State, where our farm and our first customers are. Starting in one state let us keep a promise that matters most for food and livestock: goods reach you fresh, on short roads, and a real person can answer for every order. Delivering only within Ekiti at first meant:

  • Fresher goods. Short journeys mean vegetables, eggs and meat spend less time on the road.
  • Healthier livestock. Less travel means less stress and fewer losses for live animals.
  • Reliable service. We could check quality, confirm every order by phone and fix problems quickly.
  • Lower cost. Short routes kept delivery fees low for everyone.

Why we now deliver to neighbouring states

Customers in Osun, Ondo, Kwara, Kogi and the states beyond asked for the same fresh produce. These states share borders and roads with Ekiti, so we can reach them while keeping the advantages above.

  • Still close to the farm. Neighbouring states are a short drive away, so freshness is protected.
  • One marketplace, more choice. More buyers can enjoy yam, rice, garri, peppers, eggs, pork, broilers and cattle from one place.
  • GPS tracking everywhere. Follow your order on the map, whichever state you are in.
  • Same protection. Buyer Protection and the 24-hour claims desk apply in every state we deliver to. Read how you are protected.
  • Growing fairly. A bigger customer base means more demand for our farmers' produce.

How delivery works

  1. Place your order and pick your state, town or LGA, and delivery date.
  2. We call you to confirm the order and the delivery plan.
  3. Your goods are packed and sent out. Follow them on Track order.
  4. Check your goods on arrival and pay if you chose pay on delivery.
Delivery times

Ekiti deliveries are usually quickest. Other states take a little longer depending on the route, and we confirm your delivery date on the confirmation call.
