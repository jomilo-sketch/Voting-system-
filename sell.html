Are you a farmer?

Start listing today

List your produce or livestock for free and reach buyers across Ekiti and the neighbouring states. This page explains the fees and how listing works, then lets you apply.

What it costs

₦0 to list

No sign-up fee, no monthly fee and no listing fee.

You keep 100%

You keep 100% of the price you set for your goods.

Buyer pays the fee

A 5% service fee is added at checkout and paid by the buyer, never by the farmer. Delivery is charged to the buyer too.

How you get listed

  1. Apply. Fill in the form below, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you sell and where you are.
  2. We check. Every seller is checked before listing. We look at your produce or animals, and livestock needs an up-to-date vet health certificate.
  3. We list. We set up your listing with clear pictures, the units or weights buyers can choose, your price and your stock.
  4. Orders arrive. We confirm each order with the buyer, arrange delivery with GPS tracking, and handle buyer questions and claims.
  5. You get paid. We agree the payment schedule with you when you are verified, and you can see your sales with us at any time.

What to have ready

  • What you sell, how much you have, and your price per unit or per kg.
  • Your location (town and LGA).
  • For livestock: breed, age, weight range and a vet health certificate.
  • A phone number we can reach you on.
