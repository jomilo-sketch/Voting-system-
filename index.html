Quick answers

How fast is delivery?

Ekiti is usually within about 24 hours. Other states take a little longer — we confirm your date on the confirmation call.

How long do I have to report a problem?

24 hours from delivery. Message us or log a complaint with your order reference and photos.

How can I pay?

Pay on delivery, bank transfer, or card through Paystack — whatever is shown at checkout.

I forgot my password

On the login page choose “Forgot password” and we send a 4-digit code by email or phone.
